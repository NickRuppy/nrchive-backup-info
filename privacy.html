NRCHIVE Hetzner Backups

Privacy information

Updated October 2, 2026. This tool is operated by Nick Rupprechter solely for his personal research backups.

Google access and use

The tool requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create its private backup folder, upload encrypted archives, list and download its own files for verification and recovery, and remove older backup files after a replacement is verified. It does not request permission to read unrelated Drive files or other Google services.

Storage and protection

Archive contents and filenames are encrypted before upload. Google stores these encrypted files in the operator's Drive account. The uploader runs on Hetzner and uses a restricted, encrypted authorization configuration for unattended access. The backup decryption secret is retained separately for recovery. The planned retention is seven daily recovery points.

Running-server compromise can expose its credentials or encryption keys. These backups are recovery copies, not an immutable offline vault.

Sharing and revocation

The tool does not publish backup contents or use Google user data for advertising, analytics, model training, or a public service. No other users are granted access to its private backup folder by this workflow. Google and Hetzner provide the storage and hosting services described above.

The operator can revoke the application's authorization in Google Account settings and remove its backup files from Drive. Revocation stops future access but does not automatically delete existing archives.
