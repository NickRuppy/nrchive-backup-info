NRCHIVE Hetzner Backups

A personal backup tool operated by Nick Rupprechter for his own research workflow. The workflow runs on his Hetzner server and is designed to keep encrypted recovery copies in his Google Drive.

The Google Drive permission requested is drive.file. The uploader creates a private backup folder and manages the files it creates. It does not request permission to read unrelated Drive files, Gmail, contacts, or calendars.

This is not a public service or an application offered to other users.
